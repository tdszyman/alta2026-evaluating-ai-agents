# Primary references

Curated list from organiser notes. Secondary items are bias / judge-fairness related.

- Chen et al. (2026) *Emerging Trends in AI Agent Evaluation*. https://sage.cs.princeton.edu/trends/emerging-trends-paper.pdf
- Jia et al. (2025) *What is Your Agent’s GPA? A Framework for Evaluating Agent Goal-Plan-Action Alignment*. https://arxiv.org/html/2510.08847v1

- Voss. (2026) *Where agent evals are going: Agent-as-a-Judge.* https://arize.com/blog/agent-as-a-judge-agentic-evaluation/

- Sternbach. (2026) *How to scale agentic evaluation: lessons from 200,000 SWE-bench runs.* https://www.ai21.com/blog/scaling-agentic-evaluation-swe-bench/
